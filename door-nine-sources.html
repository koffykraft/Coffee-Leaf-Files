Door Nine - Sources

Sources and Reading List

The main research and regulatory sources used in Door Nine.

How sources are usedReading list

Sources appear close to the statements they support so the reader does not have to guess which paper a claim came from. This page gathers the same sources in one place.

Evidence rule

Human, animal, cell, laboratory, review, traditional and regulatory sources are kept separate. A finding is described at the level at which it was actually observed.
