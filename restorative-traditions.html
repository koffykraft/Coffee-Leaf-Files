Door Nine - Traditions

Restorative Traditions

How to take traditional reports seriously without turning them into claims they never made.

Why tradition mattersChemoEngereHow to readSources

Traditional use answers a different question from laboratory science. It tells us what people prepare, how they use it, what meaning it has, and what they say they experience.

Door Two already contains the fuller cultural histories and preparation details. This page only explains how those reports relate to questions about the body.

Chemo

A 2026 Tepi study documented household preparation, ingredients, social use and cultural meaning. Participants used descriptions that included warming, refreshing, invigorating and restorative.

What this tells us

These are documented human experiences and cultural meanings. They show how the beverage lives in a community.

What it does not tell us

The study did not test whether Chemo produces a clinical restorative effect.

For the full Buna treatment of the tradition, see Chemo.

Source: Awoke, Gizaw and Tilahun, 2026.

Coffee leaf brew and Engere

A 2026 study documented coffee leaf brew and Engere among Gofa communities, including preparation, ingredients, consumption patterns and perceived medicinal values.

What this tells us

It records real household practice and what people themselves believe the drinks do for them.

Keep two things separate

A perceived medicinal value is evidence of belief and experience. It is not the same as a measured medical outcome.

For the full Buna treatment of the tradition, see Engere.

Source: Yohannis et al., Discover Food, 2026.

A simple way to read traditional evidence

1

What do people make?

Record the leaf, process, ingredients and preparation.

2

How do they use it?

Record when, why and with whom it is consumed.

3

What do they say they experience?

Keep their own language where possible.

4

What could modern research test?

Only after documenting the tradition should researchers ask whether a measurable physiological observation corresponds with those reports.
