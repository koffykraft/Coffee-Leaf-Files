Door Nine · Research Frontier

What we still do not know.

The most useful health-related coffee-leaf page may be the one that records what has not yet been demonstrated.

Human questions

  • Does ordinary coffee-leaf infusion alter the human microbiome?
  • How much mangiferin and chlorogenic acid is actually absorbed after a defined cup?
  • Which circulating or urinary metabolites appear after consumption?
  • Does repeated daily consumption produce measurable physiological change?
  • Can the 2025 cortisol observation be replicated in a larger controlled trial?

Processing questions

  • Does fermentation improve or reduce useful bioaccessibility?
  • How do green, oxidised, roasted, dark-fermented and smoked leaf preparations differ after digestion?
  • Does cinnamon-wood smoking materially change safety, aroma chemistry or biological plausibility?
  • How does leaf maturity interact with cultivar, shade, season and processing?
  • How different are Arabica and Robusta finished beverages at matched brew strength?

The missing bridge study

Priority research design

Define the leaf and process → chemically characterise the finished beverage → use simulated digestion to map transformation → measure microbial metabolism where appropriate → then test a defined beverage in a controlled human study.

This would connect Buna's existing plant-to-cup processing framework with the Post-Cup Landscape instead of treating “health” as a separate marketing layer.
