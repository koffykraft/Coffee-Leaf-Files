“Health benefits” needs a more careful question.

This former reference page has been replaced by Buna’s evidence-mapped Coffee Leaf & Human Biology section. Coffee-leaf research includes promising compositional, cell, animal and early human findings, but these evidence types must not be presented as equivalent.

Why this page changed

Earlier wording on this URL overstated what individual studies could establish. Buna now separates human intervention, observational, animal, cell, in-vitro, compositional and traditional evidence and states explicitly when a clinical effect has not been demonstrated.
