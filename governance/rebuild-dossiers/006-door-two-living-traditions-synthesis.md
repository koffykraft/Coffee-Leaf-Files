# Buna Door Two Synthesis — Living Traditions

Audit state: FOUR TRADITION FAMILIES REVIEWED / ARCHITECTURE DERIVED

Families included:
- Engere
- Kuti
- Chemo
- Kawa Daun

This is an architectural synthesis, not a validity sign-off. Each family retains an open source/claim verification queue.

## 1. What Door Two is supposed to do

Door Two is the reader's entrance into living coffee-leaf traditions.

It should answer:

- Where are coffee leaves actually used as beverages in documented community practice?
- How do those traditions differ?
- What do the sources directly document?
- What do communities report or believe?
- What remains interpretation, adaptation or unknown?

Door Two should not behave like an encyclopaedia index on first contact. It should feel like entering a room containing four traditions, each of which can be viewed at increasing depth.

## 2. Ordinary-reader structure

The Door itself is the threshold.

### Door landing — CHOOSE A TRADITION

The landing page should be simple enough to understand in under a minute.

Four cards only:

- Engere — Ethiopia — coffee leaf brew with milk
- Kuti — Harar, Ethiopia — dried/fallen leaf tradition
- Chemo — Southwestern Ethiopia — coffee leaf with herbs/spices and heat preparation
- Kawa Daun — West Sumatra — heat/smoke processed coffee leaf

Each card contains:
- place;
- one-sentence description;
- one distinguishing feature;
- a small evidence label such as `Documented ethnographic tradition`;
- one action: **Walk in**.

No detailed health claims, chemistry, colonial history, percentages or long ingredient lists on the Door landing.

## 3. Common family architecture

Every tradition uses the same depth progression.

### LEVEL 1 — LOOK

Natural label: **What is it?**

Purpose:
- ordinary reader;
- short, complete orientation;
- reader may leave satisfied.

Common structure:
1. tradition name;
2. place and community;
3. plain-language one-sentence definition;
4. 3 verified distinguishing facts;
5. simple documented preparation flow where evidence supports it;
6. one box: `What the source reports` where a culturally important use needs mention;
7. one short `What we do not know / should not assume` note where necessary;
8. actions:
   - `Back to Living Traditions`
   - `Understand more →`

Do not include:
- full bibliography;
- study methods table;
- phytochemistry;
- mechanism speculation;
- detailed health claims;
- long timelines;
- multiple peer navigation tabs;
- schoolbook/visual-guide links as separate destinations.

### LEVEL 2 — UNDERSTAND

Natural label: **Understand the tradition**

Purpose:
- cultural and practical context;
- documented variation;
- what the tradition means in household/community life.

Common structure:
1. concise reminder of what the tradition is — no full Level 1 repetition;
2. place / community / livelihood context;
3. documented preparation patterns and variations;
4. ingredients/material culture only where relevant;
5. social/ritual/household context;
6. reported uses clearly labelled as `Community report` or equivalent;
7. interpretation boxes explicitly labelled;
8. comparison to another tradition only where it clarifies rather than distracts;
9. actions:
   - `← What is it?`
   - `Evidence & sources →`
   - `Back to Living Traditions`.

Level 2 may include compact maps, diagrams and tables if they simplify understanding.

### LEVEL 3 — EXAMINE

Natural label: **Evidence & sources**

Purpose:
- serious inquiry;
- source provenance;
- methods;
- uncertainty;
- chemistry where relevant;
- full references.

Common structure:
1. study/source basis;
2. sample, location, dates and method;
3. source-by-source factual record;
4. detailed preparation evidence;
5. botanical/chemical material only when sourced;
6. evidence table:
   - documented;
   - community report;
   - interpretation;
   - hypothesis;
   - unknown;
7. conflicting or incomplete evidence;
8. research gaps;
9. complete bibliography / source links;
10. actions:
   - `← Understand the tradition`
   - `Back to Living Traditions`.

## 4. How special content fits without creating extra depth levels

### Preparation / recipes

Preparation is a module, not automatically a peer page.

At Level 1:
- only a simple documented flow if the source supports it.

At Level 2:
- documented household variants.

At Level 3:
- exact source measurements, producer-specific methods, technical notes, reconstruction caveats.

Any KoffyKraft/Buna modern recipe must be labelled `Reconstruction` or `Experiment`, never silently presented as the traditional method.

### Plants / spices

If a tradition has many ingredients, show a short plain-language selection at Level 2.

Full botanical identities and quantities belong at Level 3.

If plant reference becomes useful across several Doors, the whole-library synthesis may later create a reusable botanical reference archetype. Door Two itself should not create one-off peer pages unless necessary.

### Visual guide

A visual guide is an alternate presentation of accepted material, not a separate knowledge path.

Possible UI:
- `View as diagram`
- `Simple preparation view`

It must use the same approved claims as the text layer.

### Schoolbook

Schoolbook pedagogy may inspire tone and sequencing but should not survive as parallel canonical pages if the same knowledge is already represented by Look/Understand/Examine.

## 5. Shared evidence language

Door Two needs simple evidence labels ordinary readers can understand.

Recommended set:

**Documented** — directly reported/observed in the cited source.

**People report** — community belief, use or experience recorded by researchers.

**Interpretation** — Buna's explanation or connection based on evidence but not directly established by the source.

**Hypothesis** — plausible mechanism or research question not yet established.

**Unknown** — evidence currently insufficient.

Avoid academic label overload on Level 1. Full evidence taxonomy can appear at Level 3.

## 6. Shared design skeleton

All four traditions should feel related without becoming visually identical.

### Shared structural shell
- one global Buna navigation;
- breadcrumb or Door context;
- tradition identity;
- one depth indicator;
- content;
- one outward and one inward route.

### Depth indicator

A compact three-state control, not six horizontal tabs:

`LOOK  ·  UNDERSTAND  ·  EXAMINE`

On mobile this may be rendered vertically or as one current-state label with `Deeper →` / `← Simpler` controls.

### Family personality

Each tradition may have restrained visual character—e.g. colour/accent/illustration—but layout grammar and evidence grammar remain common.

Distinct visual identity must never imply different standards of truth.

## 8. Cross-family distinctions that must remain visible

The shared architecture must not flatten the traditions.

### Engere
Distinctive issue: milk + coffee-leaf brew and household reported uses/tolerance. High risk of turning respondent reports into therapeutic claims.

### Kuti
Distinctive issue: leaf maturity/fallen-leaf narrative, Harari context and caffeine claims. High risk of mechanistic and child-safety overclaiming.

### Chemo
Distinctive issue: herbs/spices, heat/roasting variation, food/hospitality context. High risk of converting observed preparation into universal rules and inferred chemistry.

### Kawa Daun
Distinctive issue: smoke/heat processing and colonial-era history. High risk of romanticised resistance narrative and generalising producer-specific methods.

## 9. Reader journey test

A successful Door Two journey should work like this:

**Library**
→ **Door Two — Living Traditions**
→ sees four traditions
→ chooses Kuti
→ reads `What is Kuti?`
→ can leave with a coherent understanding
→ or chooses `Understand more`
→ sees Harari household/cultural context and preparation variation
→ can leave
→ or chooses `Evidence & sources`
→ sees source methods, chemistry questions, evidence limits and bibliography.

At no point should the reader need to understand why a Schoolbook, Visual Guide, Recipe, Deep Reference and Fascinating page all coexist.

## 10. Governing structural principle

Door Two demonstrates the general Buna rebuild rule:

> Depth is about increasing resolution, not increasing drama and not increasing the number of pages.

Basic, Intermediate and Advanced must tell the same truth at different resolutions.

The reader who stops at Basic should not receive a different conclusion from the reader who reaches Advanced.

The deeper level may qualify, complicate or narrow the simpler statement, but it must not overturn it because the simple statement was overclaimed.
