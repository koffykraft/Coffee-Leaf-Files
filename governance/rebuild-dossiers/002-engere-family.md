## 1. What the Engere family should do

A rebuilt Engere family needs to answer four separate questions:

1. **What is Engere?** — documented preparation, geography, household context.
2. **What did the field research actually report?** — survey/observation findings, with clear distinction between report and proof.
3. **How is it prepared in the documented tradition?** — descriptive ethnographic preparation, not invented health prescription.
4. **What is interpretation versus established evidence?** — why milk, spices, work context and household care may matter without converting plausibility into mechanism or clinical effect.

The current eight-page family repeats these questions many times and has major differences in evidence discipline.

## 2. Family-level message-engine finding

The Engere corpus contains at least three editorial generations:

### Generation A — reader-friendly narrative

`engere-intro.html` and `engere.html`

Strength: accessible and memorable.
Risk: rhetorical statements often sit beside survey findings without sufficiently distinguishing fact, household report and interpretation.

### Generation B — evidence-aware reference

`engere-deep.html` and especially `engere-people-work-context.html`

Strength: explicitly distinguishes documented findings from interpretation and contains useful caution language.
Risk: still includes unsourced or under-sourced ethnobotanical, phytochemical and historical synthesis that requires proposition-level verification.

### Generation C — prescriptive schoolbook/visual material

`engere-schoolbook-guide-part-1.html`, `engere-schoolbook-guide-part-2.html`, `engere-visual-pictogram-guide.html`, and portions of `engere-recipe.html`.

Risk: these pages convert reported use and observational findings into causal, therapeutic, safety and preparation prescriptions. They should not be used as authoritative rebuild copy without complete re-verification.

## 3. `engere-intro.html`

### Current role

Friendly first encounter.

### Useful material

- Engere is presented as coffee-leaf brew combined with fresh cow's milk.
- Gofa Zone / South Ethiopia context.
- household variation in spices/sweeteners.
- simple explanation that milk is structurally central to Engere.
- clear invitation to continue deeper.

### Meaning risks

#### Rhetorical overstatement

"The part of the coffee plant everyone throws away" is not a factual description of global coffee-leaf use.

Disposition: **REWRITE** as a contrast between bean-centred commercial coffee and documented leaf use.

#### Duration claim

"made this way ... for longer than anyone in the study bothered to count" is rhetoric presented as history.

Disposition: **REMOVE OR SOURCE-VERIFY**.

#### Nourishment / vulnerable-group implication

The page says Engere is used "for mothers who have just given birth" and "children who need something nourishing and gentle."

These must be separated into:
- documented respondent use/report;
- nutritional inference;
- clinical implication.

Disposition: **REWRITE AS REPORTED USE ONLY unless independent evidence supports more.**

#### Milk tolerance claim

"makes it suitable for people who cannot tolerate the plain brew" converts observational household reporting into suitability advice.

Disposition: **REMOVE PRESCRIPTIVE LANGUAGE**.

#### Absolute preparation rules

"always served hot", "always made fresh", "never stored", "never reheated" require direct source support and must be framed as documented practice, not universal food-safety law.

Disposition: **SOURCE-VERIFY + REFRAME AS DOCUMENTED PRACTICE**.

### Rebuild disposition

**KEEP ROLE / REWRITE COPY.**

The rebuilt intro should be substantially shorter and contain no physiological or safety mechanism.

## 4. `engere.html` — What's Fascinating

### Useful material

- attempts to surface notable field-study observations;
- contrast between plain coffee-leaf brew and Engere;
- household-use context;
- spice diversity.

### Major message risks

#### "100%" safety splash

The page turns a survey report of no health-related problems linked to Engere into:

- "zero health problems";
- "Not one adverse event";
- a contrast implying the preparation itself is proven safer.

A self-reported cross-sectional field study is not a controlled safety trial and "no reported health problems" is not equivalent to "zero adverse events" in a clinical sense.

Disposition: **REWRITE COMPLETELY.**

#### "The milk changes everything"

This is causal framing.

Disposition: **REMOVE AS CLAIM.** If retained as editorial language, it must refer only to composition/sensory structure, not safety or physiology.

#### Proposed mechanism presented too vividly

The page describes milk buffering acidity, proteins binding phenolics, fat coating the stomach lining, then says the study "confirms the outcome".

This overstates what an observational household study can establish.

Disposition: **MOVE TO A RESEARCH QUESTION BOX, not explanatory fact.**

#### Restorative categories

"Chemo sustains. Engere restores." is a strong interpretive slogan derived from reported cultural use.

Disposition: **DO NOT USE AS FACTUAL SUMMARY.** Could be quoted as a site interpretation only if clearly labelled and useful.

### Rebuild disposition

**MERGE selected observations into the canonical Engere page; retire this as a separate rhetorical page unless the final architecture needs an explicit Findings page.**

## 5. `engere-deep.html`

### Useful material

- best current attempt at a serious reference page;
- explicit contents structure;
- dedicated "what should not be claimed" section;
- full ingredient and method records;
- source block;
- separation of evidence, warning and interpretation boxes.

### Risks requiring verification

- "complete utilisation knowledge" is presented as an ethnobotanical concept and generalisation without immediate source support;
- statements about Coffea arabica native range;
- mangiferin/chlorogenic acid/caffeine/tannin/lipid maturity claims;
- independent-development / convergence interpretation across Ethiopia, Indonesia and Yemen;
- claims about mature-leaf selection motivations;
- broad claims that leaf use predates bean commodity spread.

These may be correct, but they need citations independent of the Yohannis household study.

### Rebuild disposition

**KEEP AS PRIMARY SOURCE MATERIAL FOR A FUTURE REFERENCE PAGE, WITH PROPOSITION-LEVEL CITATIONS.**

## 6. `engere-people-work-context.html`

### Strengths

This is the strongest message-engine page in the current Engere family.

It explicitly says:
- the study documents reported use, not clinical benefit;
- livelihood connections are interpretation;
- stamina/lactation/disease-treatment claims should not be made;
- milk does not scientifically neutralise all risk;
- community invention/history should not be asserted without evidence.

This distinction is exactly what the rebuilt library needs.

### Remaining risks

- regional livelihood material must have its own sources, not merely the Engere paper;
- phrases such as "fits working households" are interpretation and must remain labelled;
- nutrition language about milk is composition-level, not proof of recovery effect.

### Rebuild disposition

**KEEP CORE / MERGE INTO ENGERE CONTEXT OR REFERENCE PAGE.**

This page should strongly influence the message-engine architecture of the rebuilt tradition pages.

## 7. `engere-recipe.html`

### Role problem

The page presents ethnographic preparation as a scalable recipe/instruction system with serving sizes, ratios, equipment rules, milk recommendations, quality standards and storage guidance.

That is a different epistemic act from documenting a tradition.

### Risks

- precise ratios and preparation times need traceable source support;
- universal rules about reheating/storage require food-safety evidence;
- "preferred/acceptable/avoid" milk categories are recommendations, not ethnography;
- scaling household ethnographic preparations into recipe quantities can create invented precision;
- if raw/unpasteurised milk is recommended anywhere in the family, that requires a separate food-safety review and should not be carried into general public instruction by default.

### Rebuild disposition

**SPLIT.**

Future structure should distinguish:
1. **Documented preparation** — what the field source reports;
2. **KoffyKraft reconstruction / experimental preparation** — if Buna chooses to test or adapt it, clearly labelled as reconstruction, with modern food-safety rules.

Do not merge those two categories.

## 8. `engere-schoolbook-guide-part-1.html`

### Critical message-engine failures

The page states or teaches:
- Engere "strengthens mothers, supports recovery, and buffers acidity";
- it is a "targeted intervention";
- it is a "ritualistic strength-enhancing beverage";
- it is "valued for therapeutic purposes";
- milk has a scientifically explained buffer mechanism;
- fresh milk prevents gastric effects;
- a "safety profile" is linked directly to preparation;
- methods are classified by therapeutic use.

These are not appropriate transformations of an observational ethnobotanical survey into schoolbook fact.

### Rebuild disposition

**ARCHIVE — CLAIM QUARANTINE.**

Do not migrate prose directly. Extract only:
- possibly useful pedagogical sequencing;
- documented ingredient/method data after source verification;
- neutral illustrations/layout ideas.

## 10. `engere-visual-pictogram-guide.html`

### Major risks

The visual guide is especially dangerous because strong claims are made visually and prescriptively:

- "Best For" lactating mothers, children and sensitive digestion;
- instruction to use fresh unpasteurised cow's milk;
- "enhanced therapeutic benefit";
- "All Three Methods Are Safe";
- "100% ... ZERO health problems";
- causal "milk buffer effect protects" language;
- a "Health Benefits" section that converts respondent beliefs/uses into benefit cards.

Visual presentation increases the authority and memorability of these claims.

### Rebuild disposition

**RETIRE FROM CURRENT PUBLIC EDUCATION / ARCHIVE FOR SOURCE MINING.**

A future visual Engere guide can exist, but it must be rebuilt from verified descriptive ethnography and modern food-safety principles.

## 11. Canonical Engere architecture for the rebuild

Do not rebuild eight pages by default.

Recommended content model after source verification:

### A. Engere — Overview

- what it is;
- where documented;
- core ingredients;
- household/cultural context;
- key field-study findings clearly labelled as respondent reports;
- links to Reference and Preparation.

### B. Engere — Evidence & Context

- study design and sample;
- exact reported preparation types;
- exact reported uses/beliefs;
- people/work/livelihood context;
- ingredient record;
- what is established / not established;
- cross-links to broader coffee-leaf science where relevant.

### C. Engere — Documented Preparation

- ethnographic preparation as reported;
- variants and ingredients;
- no invented therapeutic method selection;
- no general safety claims;
- no raw-milk recommendation for modern readers without explicit safety treatment.

### D. Optional visual explainer

Only after the above is validity-approved. Visuals must distinguish:
- documented practice;
- reported belief;
- interpretation;
- modern reconstruction.

## 12. What survives

### Strong candidates

- the existence and identity of Engere as a milk-combined coffee-leaf preparation;
- Gofa Zone / South Ethiopia field-study context;
- three documented preparation types if confirmed by source;
- ingredient diversity;
- household/cultural use descriptions;
- people/work context with explicit interpretation labels;
- evidence-discipline language from the deep/context pages.

### Does not survive automatically

- therapeutic labels;
- maternal/child recommendations;
- "all methods safe";
- causal milk-buffer mechanism;
- universal storage/reheating rules;
- unpasteurised milk instructions;
- clinical-sounding adverse-event language;
- claims that traditional reported use proves effect;
- "Chemo sustains / Engere restores" as fact;
- percentages presented as health efficacy without exact denominator/context;
- invented precision in recipe scaling.

## 13. Validity audit queue created by Engere

Before content approval, verify against original sources:

1. exact household sample and denominators for Engere use;
2. exact meaning of "100% no health-related problems" and which respondents it covers;
3. exact 35.6% plain-brew symptom figure and wording;
4. reported lactation, strength, postpartum, pain and medicinal-use percentages;
5. three preparation-type percentages;
6. milk ratios, serving sizes, leaf amounts and preparation times;
7. whether storage/reheating rules are documented at all;
8. ingredient/spice list;
9. any raw/unpasteurised milk description versus recommendation;
10. regional livelihood claims and their independent sources;
11. phytochemical/mangiferin claims and independent sources;
12. historical claims about coffee-leaf use outside the Yohannis study.

## 14. Door Two architectural implication

The present four-step pattern — Intro / Fascinating / Deep / Recipe — is not automatically the rebuild model. Engere shows that it encourages rhetorical escalation and repeated retelling.

The whole Door Two audit must determine whether each tradition is better represented by a common three-part model:

**Overview → Evidence & Context → Documented Preparation**

with optional visual/educational material generated from those approved sources rather than maintained as separate competing truth layers.
