## 1. Intended role

Door Nine should help an ordinary reader understand what research can and cannot currently say about coffee leaf after it enters the human body.

Its governing question is:

> What happens after the cup, and how far does the evidence actually go?

Unlike several older Doors, Door Nine already has a strong evidence discipline. The rebuild should preserve that discipline while reducing the amount of material presented as peer choices at the first threshold.

## 2. What the current Door gets right

Several principles should become Library-wide standards:

- leaf composition is not cup composition;
- cup composition is not human exposure;
- animal evidence remains animal evidence;
- cell evidence remains cell evidence;
- traditional report remains traditional report;
- extract dose is not silently equated with an ordinary drink;
- the finished product and serving matter;
- uncertainty is explained in ordinary language;
- sources are placed close to the claims they support;
- research gaps are treated as useful knowledge rather than embarrassment.

The overview's rule — `No result is promoted above the level at which it was observed` — is an excellent message-engine rule for the entire Buna rebuild.

## 3. Three-depth architecture

### Level 1 — LOOK: After the cup

Purpose: a person with no biology background understands the basic journey and current state of evidence.

Suggested page:

**What happens after you drink coffee leaf?**

Structure:
1. Cup enters digestion.
2. Brewing determines what was actually swallowed.
3. Digestion and gut microbes may change compounds.
4. The intestinal barrier controls what reaches the body.
5. The liver and other tissues further change what is absorbed.
6. Human effects require human evidence.
7. Safety depends on dose/product.

Then one simple evidence ladder:
- lab/cell;
- animal;
- people.

And a concise summary:
- chemistry knowledge: substantial;
- gut/metabolic evidence: promising but mostly preclinical;
- stress/relaxation: early human evidence;
- traditional restorative use: documented as report;
- treatment claims: not established.

Actions:
- `Understand the evidence →`
- `Safety and dose →` as a prominent secondary route.

The current `coffee-leaf-human-biology.html` is a strong source for this Level 1 but should be shorter and less directory-like.

### Level 2 — UNDERSTAND: The biological pathways

Rather than seven equal page cards, organize into a few human questions.

Suggested modules:

#### A. From cup to body
Extraction, digestion, bioaccessibility, bioavailability, microbes, absorption.
Source pool: `post-cup-landscape.html`.

#### B. Gut, barrier and metabolism
Combine the ordinary-reader explanations now split between `gut-barrier-microbiome.html` and `metabolic-signals.html`.
Explain:
- intestinal barrier;
- microbiome;
- SCFAs;
- gut-liver axis;
- enzyme tests;
- what animal/in-vitro signals mean.

#### C. Human experience
Combine the human-response story with restorative-tradition context where useful:
- cortisol study;
- sensory response;
- community reports;
- why perceived strengthening/restoration is a research question rather than clinical result.

#### D. Safety and variability
Keep this highly visible. It should not be buried at the end of a long sequence.

Each module must be readable independently.

### Level 3 — EXAMINE: Studies, dose, methods and sources

Advanced research layer containing:
- study-by-study summaries;
- exact models and sample types;
- extract doses;
- processing/preparation details;
- measured outcomes;
- limitations;
- source links;
- regulatory sources;
- research gaps;
- proposed bridge-study design.

`door-nine-sources.html` becomes part of this evidence layer rather than a peer destination ordinary readers must discover separately.

Potential refinement:

Disposition: SPLIT simple biology → UNDERSTAND; taxa/dose/study detail → EXAMINE.

### `metabolic-signals.html`

Good distinction between enzyme assay and human outcome.

For ordinary readers, alpha-amylase/alpha-glucosidase/lipase can be explained more compactly as digestive-enzyme laboratory tests, with individual names expanded at Level 3.

The gut-liver-metabolism explanation should combine with the Gut module rather than form a separate first-level destination.

Disposition: MERGE → UNDERSTAND; retain detailed assay/study material → EXAMINE.

### `rest-stress-response.html`

Generally disciplined:
- cortisol is not simplified to a `stress chemical`;
- one salivary measure is not treated as daily-life stress proof;
- GABA in food is not equated with CNS GABA effect;
- sensory pleasure and pharmacology are separated.

High-priority confirmation remains necessary for exact 2025 study design, preparation conditions, reported cortisol direction/magnitude and participant context.

Disposition: KEEP WITH EDIT → UNDERSTAND / EXAMINE.

### `restorative-traditions.html`

Strong bridge between ethnography and research questions.

It correctly states that community use/reports do not establish clinical benefit and that complete preparations such as Engere should be studied as whole food matrices rather than attributing everything to the leaf.

This material overlaps Door Two. Door Nine should only retain the **research-translation function**; full cultural detail belongs to Door Two.

Disposition: MERGE selected material into Human Experience; link to Door Two for tradition context.

### `safety-dose-variability.html`

Important enough that safety should be accessible from every depth.

Strong principles:
- safety is exposure-specific;
- caffeine varies by cultivar/leaf/process;
- product testing beats borrowed values;
- infusion ≠ extract ≠ whole-leaf food;
- fermentation/smoking/whole leaf create additional safety questions;
- agricultural contaminants matter.

Source-level verification remains essential for EU specifications, review wording and EGCG thresholds.

Disposition: KEEP WITH EDIT; prominent UNDERSTAND module + advanced regulatory/source detail.

### `research-gaps.html`

Excellent advanced function. It tells the reader what evidence is missing and proposes a coherent bridge study.

This page should not be a required ordinary-reader stop, but it should survive in Level 3 nearly in spirit.

Disposition: KEEP / EDIT → EXAMINE.

### `door-nine-sources.html`

Good evidence-type separation but currently only a reading list.

Rebuild it into a source registry where each study states:
- evidence type;
- material/preparation;
- model/population;
- question;
- major findings;
- what it cannot establish;
- claims/pages using it.

Disposition: EXPAND → EXAMINE/source registry.

## 6. Ordinary-reader evidence language

Door Nine's current evidence labels can be simplified without losing honesty.

### LOOK
Three levels are enough:
- **In the lab**
- **In animals**
- **In people**

Traditional material can appear as:
- **People report / documented tradition**

### UNDERSTAND
Add:
- cell model;
- observational study;
- intervention study.

### EXAMINE
Full design/model/dose/source detail.

The reader should learn evidence hierarchy progressively rather than face a taxonomy before understanding the biological question.

## 7. Message-engine risks still requiring source confirmation

Even well-framed pages require exact verification before migration:

1. 2025 human study participant number/design/control details.
2. Brewing optimization range 74–81°C and 4–12 min and which preparation each value applies to.
3. Oolong total phenolics `35.61 mg GAE/g` and GABA `~0.40 mg/g` units/sample basis.
4. Exact cortisol result, timing, denominator and statistical context.
5. 2026 mouse extract doses `100/200 mg/kg` and outcome directions.
6. microbiome taxa and Firmicutes/Bacteroidetes interpretation.
7. d-lactate/tight-junction marker terminology.
8. 2023 rat model extract identity and exact measured outcomes.
9. 2024 cell-fraction findings.
10. 2026 dark-tea assay details and whether statements generalise across fermentation stages.
11. safety-review wording about caffeine and overall risk.
12. EGCG threshold/context and exact EFSA basis.
13. EU 2020/917 and 2023/931 product specifications/uses.
14. whether references to `ordinary brewed cup` are supported by measured exposure data or are correctly stated as unknown.
15. all wording such as `strongest`, `coherent signal`, `promising` must be calibrated to the actual body of literature.

## 8. Safety of whole-leaf and experimental products

Door Nine correctly distinguishes infusion from whole-leaf food. This distinction must become a shared Library rule.

No safety conclusion for a traditional or regulated infusion should automatically transfer to:
- leaf powder;
- cooked whole leaf;
- concentrated extract;
- strongly fermented product;
- smoked product;
- high-dose supplement-like format.

Door Seven and Door Three must consume this safety rule from Door Nine rather than maintain separate safety assumptions.
