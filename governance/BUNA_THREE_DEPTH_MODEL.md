# Buna Three-Depth Reader Architecture

Status: ACTIVE REBUILD RULE

The Buna rebuild is for ordinary readers first. Depth is offered progressively rather than imposed.

The original Door idea is retained as a structural threshold: a reader walks in, looks, and may walk out satisfied or continue deeper.

## Core rule

Every substantive knowledge family must support three degrees of separation without forcing the reader to traverse all three.

### Level 1 — LOOK

Purpose: simple orientation for an ordinary reader.

The reader should leave able to answer:
- What is this?
- Where does it come from?
- What is the one thing that makes it distinctive?
- What is directly documented versus merely suggested?
- Where can I go next if I want more?

Characteristics:
- plain language;
- short paragraphs;
- minimal jargon;
- no unnecessary chemistry;
- no dense bibliography in the reading flow;
- no therapeutic implication hidden inside attractive prose;
- one primary page job;
- a clear exit back to the Door and one clear route deeper.

Level 1 must feel complete by itself. It is not a teaser requiring Level 2.

### Level 2 — UNDERSTAND

Purpose: context and meaning.

The reader should be able to understand:
- cultural and household context;
- documented preparation patterns;
- important variation;
- why the practice matters;
- what the main source actually establishes;
- what is interpretation rather than documentation;
- comparisons with related traditions where genuinely useful.

Characteristics:
- moderate detail;
- evidence labels close to claims;
- contextual maps/tables only where they simplify understanding;
- no duplication of the Level 1 introduction;
- technical terms explained on first use;
- optional contextual cross-links;
- a clear route back outward and a clear route to Level 3.

### Level 3 — EXAMINE

Purpose: serious inquiry without requiring the whole site to read like a research paper.

May contain:
- full method documentation;
- source-by-source critique;
- chemistry and process mechanisms;
- conflicting evidence;
- study design and limitations;
- unresolved questions;
- full bibliography;
- technical tables;
- research and reconstruction notes.

Characteristics:
- accuracy over brevity;
- explicit evidence level;
- no simplification that changes meaning;
- direct distinction between published evidence, community report, Buna/Citane observation, interpretation and hypothesis;
- sources attached closely enough that claims can be checked.

## What is NOT a depth level

### Visual guide
A visual guide is a presentation mode, not a fourth depth. It must be generated from an already-audited Level 1, 2 or 3 content layer and may not introduce new claims.

### Recipe / preparation
A preparation page is not automatically advanced. It belongs at the depth justified by the evidence:
- a simple documented household preparation can be Level 1 or 2;
- reconstructed quantities, safety thresholds, equipment rules or mechanistic explanations may require Level 3 or may be excluded until verified.

### AI companion
An AI helper is an access/interface layer, not a new evidence layer. It must inherit the same evidence distinctions and must not upgrade a reported practice into a fact.

## Navigation expression

Within a Door or family, the reader should see a simple progression such as:

**Look → Understand → Examine**

Equivalent natural labels may be used where clearer, for example:
- What is it?
- Understand the tradition
- Evidence & sources

The interface must not expose six peer tabs when three depth choices are enough.

## Exit and return rule

At every depth the reader must be able to:
- return to the family/Door;
- move one level outward;
- move one level deeper;
- leave the family without losing site context.

No reader should be forced through an intermediate redirect, repeated global navigation, or a parallel alternate guide.

## Content migration rule

When rebuilding a family:
1. inventory all current pages;
2. de-duplicate propositions;
3. validate or quarantine claims;
4. assign surviving material to LOOK / UNDERSTAND / EXAMINE;
5. design page structure only after assignment;
6. generate visual modes only from accepted content;
7. archive old pages only after unique material has been captured.

## Plain-language standard

Simple does not mean vague. The preferred pattern is:

**plain statement → necessary context → evidence qualifier → optional deeper route**

Avoid rhetorical amplification where a factual statement would teach better.

## Governing test

For every family ask:

> Can an ordinary reader walk in, understand the subject without technical burden, and walk out satisfied — while a curious reader can continue inward without encountering a different or contradictory version of the truth?

If not, the architecture fails.
